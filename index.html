  • Permanent Titanium Implants

  • Complete & Partial Dentures

Broken Teeth & Jaw Trauma

Dedicated emergency trauma care for fractured teeth, broken jawbones, loose teeth management, and minor maxillofacial surgeries.

  • Treatment of Broken Jaw
  • Cracked & Broken Tooth Restoration
  • Loose Tooth Splinting

Surgical Extractions & Wisdom Teeth

Painless, safe extraction of impacted third molars (wisdom teeth), dental abscess drainage, and surgical extractions under local anesthesia.

  • Impacted Wisdom Tooth Removal
  • Surgical & Simple Extractions
  • Abscess & Infection Treatment

Professional ultrasonic plaque and tartar removal to cure bleeding gums, bad breath (halitosis), gingivitis, and gum pocket swelling.

  • Ultrasonic Scaling & Polishing
  • Bleeding & Swollen Gum Care
  • Deep Cleaning & Root Planing

Computerized RVG Digital X-Ray
